# Playtest 1 — Checklist

**Question:** is building an equation fun?

**Setup:** iPad in landscape, the PR preview (or production) added to the Home Screen, sound on.
Open the app → big orange ▶ → level 1. Levels go in order (8 in all); after the last one there is an
"all done" screen with ▶ to play again. Progress is not saved — reloading starts at the menu.

**Your role:** hand him the iPad and say as little as possible. Don't explain the rules, the tiles or
the cannon. If he asks, answer with a question ("what do you think will happen?"). Only step in if
he is stuck *and* unhappy for more than a minute or so — and note that you did.

## The levels (for you, not him)

| # | Board | What it teaches | One exact solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cannon + robot 1, no tiles | End Turn; the exact-kill celebration | End Turn |
| 2 | Cannon in the wrong lane, robot 3, tray `+2` | Moving the cannon | Move cannon to the robot's lane, 1 + 2 = 3 |
| 3 | Robot 9, tray `+3` `+5` | Chaining tiles | 1 + 3 + 5 = 9 |
| 4 | Robot 12, tray `×2` `+5` | Order matters | (1 + 5) × 2 = 12 (not 1 × 2 + 5 = 7) |
| 5 | Robot 8 standing on a `×3`, tray `+1` `+2` `×2` | A robot's own tile doesn't count | (1 + 1 + 2) × 2 = 8 |
| 6 | Robots 7 and 10 in two lanes, one cannon, tray `+4` `+6` `×2` | Firing, then moving the cannon | 1 + 6 = 7, then (1 + 4) × 2 = 10 |
| 7 | Robot 7, tray `×5` `−3` `+1` | Subtraction as a tool | (1 + 1) × 5 − 3 = 7 |
| 8 | Two cannons; robot 15 in one lane, robots 11 then 24 in another; tray `+4` `×3` `+5` `×2` `−1` `+2` | Everything; front robot first; reusing tiles | (1 + 4) × 3 = 15 and (1 + 5) × 2 − 1 = 11, then (1 + 5 + 2) × 3 = 24 |

## What to watch for

Tick or jot a word next to each as it happens; the level number helps.

**Cannon**
- [x] Does he move the cannon **unprompted** (level 2)? How long before he tries?
- [x] In level 6, does he move it between turns on his own?

**Predicting**
- [x] Does he **predict values** before End Turn — saying numbers out loud, counting on fingers,
      pointing along the lane?
- [x] Or does he place tiles and fire to see what happens (brute force)? Does that change by level 4–5?
- [x] Does he follow the number on the ball as it travels?

**Exact kills**
- [x] How does he **react to exact kills** (level 1 onward)? Smile, cheer, "again!", nothing?
- [x] Does he notice the difference between an exact kill and an overkill?
- [x] Does he try to get exact kills on purpose (e.g. undo and redo a board that would overshoot)?

**Getting stuck**
- [x] **Where does he get stuck?** Level, what he was trying, how long.
- [x] Level 4: does he discover that order matters, or give up and brute-force it?
- [x] Level 5: does he try to use the `×3` under the robot? Does he figure out why it doesn't count?
- [x] Level 7: does `−` make sense to him as something useful?
- [x] Level 8: does he realise the back robot can't be hit until the front one is gone?
- [x] Any drag trouble — tiles dropped in the wrong cell, can't pick something up, Undo needed?
- [x] Anything he taps expecting something to happen (and nothing does)?
- [x] Does he use Replay or Undo on his own?

**Wanting more**
- [x] **Does he want to keep going** after each level? After level 8, does he press ▶ to play again?
- [x] When does his attention drift, if it does?
- [x] Anything he asks for ("can I have more tiles?", "make the robot bigger")?

**Afterwards (ask him)**
- [x] Which level was the best? Which was the hardest?
- [x] What do the robots' numbers mean? What does the ball's number mean?
