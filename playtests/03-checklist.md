# Playtest 3 — Checklist

**Question:** is the shop a real choice — does spending coins feel meaningful, and does what
he buys change how he plays the next wave?

**Setup:** iPad in landscape, the PR preview (or production) already on the Home Screen, sound
on. Open the app → big orange ▶ **New Run** (fresh run — don't tap ▶ Continue if it's showing
from an earlier session; start clean). Progress now autosaves after every action, so a reload
or the app being backgrounded mid-run resumes exactly where it left off via ▶ Continue on the
menu.

**Your role:** hand him the iPad and say as little as possible. Don't explain the shop cards,
the NEW stickers, coins, or that a run can now be lost. If he asks, answer with a question
("what do you think will happen?"). Only step in if he is stuck *and* unhappy for more than a
minute or so — and note that you did.

## The waves (for you, not him)

A run in M3 is 7 waves; base HP starts at 100 and never regenerates. Clearing a wave (except
the last) shows a wave-cleared coin beat, then the shop; clearing wave 7 wins the run. A
careless run can now lose.

| Wave | What happens | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~3 robots (HP 1–3), one at a time, one lane at a time | Moving the cannon; exact kills are automatic (ball value 1) |
| 2 | ~3 robots (HP 4–10), one at a time | Placing tiles; addition; first shop after this |
| 3 | 2 robots at once (HP 4–10), then 2 more (HP 5–12) | Lane choice; shop tradeoffs; a second cannon should be in reach |
| 4 | Two lanes, then a third; HP 12–16 | Using a second cannon; bigger numbers |
| 5 | Two lanes; HP 14–30 | Trimming a big number; subtraction as a tool |
| 6 | Three lanes at once; HP 16–34 | Everything at once |
| 7 | Three lanes, then three more; HP 20–38 | The whole kit, before M4's Boss |

## What to watch for

Tick or jot a word next to each as it happens; the wave number helps.

**Shop as a choice**
- [ ] Which card does he look at **first**? Does he understand the three tile cards without
      being told?
- [ ] Does he understand the **cannon** card — does he know he just got a second lane? Does he
      then *use* it?
- [ ] Does he understand the **upgrade** card (`1 → 2`)? Does he notice the balls got bigger
      afterwards?
- [ ] Does he **spend everything immediately**, or ever save on purpose for something bigger?
- [ ] Does he tap a card he **can't afford**? Does the shake read as "no", or does he keep
      tapping?
- [ ] Does he notice the **NEW** stickers? Does he care about them at all?
- [ ] Does he **use** what he bought in the next wave, and how soon — same turn, or does it sit
      in the tray?
- [ ] Does he ask to **go back** to the shop after leaving?

**Wave-cleared, coins, loss, length**
- [ ] Does the wave-cleared **coin beat** register, or is it a screen he taps through?
- [ ] **First loss:** a run can now be lost. What happens — upset, shrug, "again"? Does the
      lose screen land as cheerful?
- [ ] Is a 7-wave run **too long**? Where does attention drift — which wave, roughly how far
      in?

**Continue and Home**
- [ ] **Continue:** close the app (or background it) mid-run — including inside the shop — and
      reopen it. Does he find ▶ Continue on his own, or tap New Run out of habit?
- [ ] Does he **tap ⌂ Home** by accident? What does he do when the menu appears?

**Wanting more**
- [ ] After the win (or lose) screen: does he want to play **another run**?
- [ ] Does the **exact-kill row** of icons mean anything to him?

**Afterwards (ask him)**
- [ ] What did you buy? Did it help?
- [ ] Which wave was the hardest? Which was the most fun?
