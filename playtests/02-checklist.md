# Playtest 2 — Checklist

**Question:** does a run hold together — do advancing robots create good pressure (moving the
cannon, choosing a lane, going for exact kills) without frustration, and does he want to go again
after winning?

**Setup:** iPad in landscape, the PR preview (or production) already on the Home Screen, sound on.
Open the app → big orange ▶ **New Run** (fresh run — don't tap ▶ Continue if it's showing from an
earlier session; start clean). Progress now autosaves after every action, so a reload or the app
being backgrounded mid-run resumes exactly where it left off via ▶ Continue on the menu.

**Your role:** hand him the iPad and say as little as possible. Don't explain the danger glow, the
♥ count-down, waves, or rewards. If he asks, answer with a question ("what do you think will
happen?"). Only step in if he is stuck *and* unhappy for more than a minute or so — and note that
you did.

## The waves (for you, not him)

A run in M2 is 3 waves; base HP starts at 100 and never regenerates. Clearing a wave (except the
last) shows a wave-cleared screen with new tiles; clearing the last wave wins the run.

| Wave | What happens | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~3 robots (HP 1–3), one at a time, one lane at a time | Moving the cannon to meet the robot; exact kills are automatic here (ball value 1 always matches) |
| 2 | ~3 robots (HP 4–10), one at a time; reward from wave 1 (`+1 +2 +3`) is already in the tray | Placing tiles to add enough damage; still one lane at a time |
| 3 | 2 robots at once (HP 4–10), then 2 more at once (HP 5–12); reward from wave 2 (`×2 +4`) also in the tray | Lane choice — one cannon, two lanes threatened |

## What to watch for

Tick or jot a word next to each as it happens; the wave number helps.

**Danger and detonations**
- [ ] Does he notice the **danger glow** (a lane pulsing red when its robot is one column from the
      base)? Does he act on it — move the cannon there?
- [ ] Reaction to a **detonation** and the ♥ count-down — upset, amused, indifferent? Does he read
      the number, or just notice it moved?
- [ ] Does he notice **waiting (ghost) robots** (translucent, off to the right) when two robots are
      due the same turn in the same lane?

**Wave-cleared and rewards**
- [ ] Does he look at the **reward tiles** popping in on the wave-cleared screen?
- [ ] Does he **use them** in the next wave, and how soon — right away, after a few turns, or does
      he need to be reminded they exist (don't remind him; just note it)?

**Lane choice (wave 3)**
- [ ] Does he pick a lane **on purpose** (aim at whichever robot is closer to the base, or about to
      be a problem), or does he thrash the cannon back and forth without a plan?
- [ ] Does he ever let one lane detonate on purpose to focus the other, or does that only happen by
      accident?

**Exact kills and effort**
- [ ] Does he still chase **exact kills** now that robots are moving (chaining tiles, undoing a
      board that would overshoot), or does he just shoot whatever's armed and move on?
- [ ] **Watch item (Playtest 1 follow-up):** does he notice that just pressing End Turn without
      moving the cannon or placing tiles ("shooting 1s") still clears wave 1 automatically, and
      chips away at waves 2–3 without much thought? Does noticing that reduce his effort — does he
      stop engaging with tiles/lane choice once he sees the run won't end quickly either way?

**Continue and Home**
- [ ] **Continue:** close the app (or background it) mid-run and reopen it — does he find ▶
      Continue on his own, or does he tap New Run out of habit?
- [ ] Does he **tap ⌂ Home** by accident? What does he do when the menu appears — look for a way
      back in, or wander off?

**Wanting more**
- [ ] After the win screen: does he want to play **another run**?
- [ ] Does the **exact-kill row** of icons on the win screen mean anything to him — does he count
      them, mention them, compare to a previous run?
- [ ] When does his attention drift, if it does? Which wave, roughly how far in?

**Afterwards (ask him)**
- [ ] What did the tiles you got after wave 1 do? Did he know before you asked?
- [ ] Which wave was the hardest? Which was the most fun?
