# Playtest 4 — Checklist

**Question:** does a complete run hold together — traits readable, stretch hard enough, Boss a
finale, and does he want to go again?

**Setup:** iPad in landscape, the PR preview (or production) already on the Home Screen, sound
on. Open the app → big orange ▶ **New Game** (fresh run). If ▶ **Keep Going** is showing from an
earlier session, do **not** tap it — tap the smaller **New Game** instead.

Why a fresh New Game: a run saved by the M3 build resumes into this 10-wave ladder through
▶ Keep Going (the save schema did not change, so the old run is not discarded). That is not
what Playtest 4 is measuring. Sound on so a wrong-parity clonk and bounce-back refill are
audible if he hits them.

**Your role:** hand him the iPad and say as little as possible. Don't explain traits, the coil,
odd vs even, Settings, hints, or that wave 10 is a Boss. If he asks, answer with a question
("what do you think will happen?"). Only step in if he is stuck *and* unhappy for more than a
minute or so — and note that you did.

## The waves (for you, not him)

A run in M4 is 10 waves; base HP starts at 100 and never regenerates. Clearing a wave (except
the last) shows a wave-cleared coin beat, then the shop; clearing wave 10 wins the run. A
careless run can lose on 8–9 and never see the Boss. Leaking the Boss is a loss.

| Wave | What happens | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~3 robots (HP 1–3), one at a time, one lane at a time | Moving the cannon; exact kills are automatic (ball value 1) |
| 2 | ~3 robots (HP 4–10), one at a time | Placing tiles; addition; first shop after this |
| 3 | 2 robots at once (HP 4–10), then 2 more (HP 5–12) | Lane choice; shop tradeoffs; a second cannon should be in reach |
| 4 | Weakness-5 on the first robot; remaining robots basic | Gold chest **n** — damage doubles when the ball matches |
| 5 | Two lanes of basics, bigger HP; no trait | Subtraction as a tool; the −N shop after this is the Bounce-back exam |
| 6 | Bounce-back on the first robot; remaining robots basic | Coil telegraph; overshoot refills instead of killing |
| 7 | Odd-only on the first robot; remaining robots basic | Odd vs even; base value 1 is odd, so the first contact can hurt |
| 8 | Procedural 3+3, mixed traits (Even-only can appear at T1) | Stretch; first-ball clonk on even; four-lane panic is later |
| 9 | Procedural 4+4+3, mixed traits, highest normal HP | Hardest stretch — he can lose here and miss the Boss |
| 10 | Boss (100–140 HP, three digits, no trait) + light escort | Finale; leaking the Boss is a loss |

Settings (gear on the menu) has a Hints toggle, **off by default**. Hints show ball value only —
no trait effects. No Sound row yet (M5).

## What to watch for

Tick or jot a word next to each as it happens; the wave number helps.

**Traits readable**
- [ ] Does he read **Weakness n** / the Bounce-back **coil** / **Odd vs Even** without being
      told? Which wave does each land on, if it lands at all?
- [ ] **Wrong-parity clonk:** confusion, or "oh, even"? Does he then reach for a tile that
      flips parity, or keep firing the same ball?
- [ ] **Bounce-back overshoot:** does he reach for `−N`? Does the coil mean anything before the
      first bounce, or only after?

**Stretch and the Boss**
- [ ] **Wave 8–9:** frustration or focus? Too long? Does he **lose here and never see the Boss**?
- [ ] **Boss:** does three-digit HP land as special? (May not appear if he lost on 8–9.)
- [ ] Does the escort at T7 trick him — does he leave the Boss lane to chase it?

**Settings / hints**
- [ ] Does he **find Settings** (the gear on the menu)? On his own, or only if he backs out to
      the menu?
- [ ] Does turning **hints on** change how he plays — does he look at the numerals, or ignore
      them?

**Wanting more**
- [ ] After the win (or lose) screen: does he want to play **another run**?
- [ ] Does the **exact-kill row** of icons mean anything to him?

**Continue and Home**
- [ ] **Keep Going:** close the app (or background it) mid-run — including inside a late shop —
      and reopen it. Does he find ▶ Keep Going on his own, or tap New Game out of habit?
- [ ] Does he **tap ⌂ Home** by accident? What does he do when the menu appears?

**Afterwards (ask him)**
- [ ] Which wave was the hardest? Which was the most fun?
- [ ] Did the robots with extra marks (gold n / coil / odd-even) make sense?
- [ ] Want to play again tomorrow?
